Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

SkillGRID helps people exchange skills through profiles, matches, scheduled sessions, and reviews. This policy explains what information the demo collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your name, email address, account mode, and institution.
  • Skills, match activity, session details, reviews, reports, and ledger activity you submit.
  • Essential session-cookie data needed to keep you signed in.

How we use information

We use this information to provide accounts, matching, scheduling, moderation, reviews, and account security. The sandbox payment feature is a demonstration and does not process live payment credentials.

Sharing and retention

Profile and marketplace information may be visible to other SkillGRID users as part of the service. We do not sell personal information. This capstone deployment uses demo infrastructure, and data may be reset or removed without notice.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. Contact the site owner to request correction or deletion of account data where supported by the deployment.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the SkillGRID project administrator through the associated project repository.
