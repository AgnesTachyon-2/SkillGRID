SkillGRID

Trade skills, not just cash.

SkillGRID is a peer-to-peer marketplace where you earn credits by teaching what you know and spend them learning what you want — matched directly, or through multi-person chain swaps when a direct trade isn't available.

Direct & Chain Matching
