Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

SkillGRID uses a small number of cookies and browser storage items to operate the demo and remember your choices.

Essential session cookie

When you sign in, the server uses an HTTP-only session cookie to recognize your authenticated requests. This cookie is required for account features and expires according to the session settings.

Consent preference

After you select Accept on the cookie banner, SkillGRID stores your choice in browser local storage under skillgrid-cookie-consent. This preference is not used for advertising or tracking.

Managing cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing the session cookie will sign you out, and clearing the consent preference will show the banner again.
