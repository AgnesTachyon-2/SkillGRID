SkillGRID

Refund Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

SkillGRID currently provides a sandbox checkout for demonstration only. It does not charge real money, connect to a live payment provider, or store payment credentials.

Sandbox transactions

Because sandbox payments are simulated, there are no real charges and no monetary refunds. A sandbox transaction may appear in the demo ledger only to demonstrate the platform workflow.

Session issues

For a disputed or incorrectly recorded demo session, contact the project administrator with the session details. The administrator may correct demo records where the deployment supports it.

Future live payments

If SkillGRID later supports live payments, this policy will be replaced with provider-specific cancellation, refund, and dispute terms before those payments are enabled.
